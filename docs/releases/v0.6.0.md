Optional cloud sync with your own Supabase project.

## New

- **Cloud sync:** Settings > Cloud sync > Sign in with Google, then **Migrate and sync**. Vehicles, fill-ups, expenses and reminders are merged both ways between your phones, the newest change wins and deletions carry over. Nothing syncs in the background: it runs only when you tap the button. The section is off unless the app was built with the URL and key of a Supabase project of yours; setup is in [docs/cloud-sync.md](https://github.com/FrancescoCastaldi/fuel-ledger-android/blob/main/docs/cloud-sync.md).
- Sign-in uses the browser and the authorization code flow with a proof key. It needs no Google Play Services.

## Changed

- The database moves to version 3: every record gets a unique id and a last-change time, and deletions are remembered until they reach the cloud. Your data is kept. Exporting a CSV backup from **Settings** before updating is still a good habit.
- The CSV import replaces the phone's records with new ones, which the next sync carries to the cloud.
