One guided scan for the whole fill-up.

## New

- **Guided scan:** open a fill-up from the widget or the dashboard's **+** and the camera starts on the dashboard. Frame the cluster: the odometer and the range are read as the phone holds steady on them. Confirm the pop-up, then frame the pump display once the delivery is over: amount, liters and price per liter are read the same way. Confirm again and the form fills; check it and save.
- **Nothing is taken without you:** every step waits for **Confirm**. **Reread** starts the step again, **Skip** leaves it out, and **Use the receipt** switches to the receipt photo.
- **Pump checks:** a pump display is accepted only when its three figures agree (liters x price equals the amount). A misread digit is not offered.

## Changed

- The receipt photo and the gallery stay available as before, from the form.

## Upgrading

Installing 0.9.0 is an update from the app, as from 0.8.1. Your data is kept. Exporting a CSV backup from **Settings** before updating is still a good habit.

## Privacy

Everything runs on the phone, like before: frames are read and dropped, and no photo is kept. The camera permission is asked only when the guided scan opens.
