Fixes to the intro video placement and the update check.

## Changed

- **Intro video on request:** the video no longer plays at startup. Open it from **Settings > About > Intro video**; tap the screen to close it.
- **Update check first:** **Settings > Updates** compares versions before downloading. A release that is not newer than the installed app is never downloaded.

## Upgrading

- Install over 0.11.0 or 0.10.1 with **Settings > Updates > Check for updates**. Your records are kept.
