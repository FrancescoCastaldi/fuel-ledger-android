APK Signature Scheme v3 and frictionless in-app update ownership.

## Changed

- **APK Signature Scheme v3:** releases are now signed with v1, v2, and v3 schemes (`enableV3Signing`), providing full key lineage metadata for Android 9 to 14 and Google Play Protect.
- **In-app update ownership:** `SelfInstaller` declares `PACKAGE_SOURCE_STORE` (Android 13+) and `setRequestUpdateOwnership(true)` (Android 14+) when committing an update session, eliminating browser download warnings when updating from **Settings > Updates**.

## Upgrading

- Open **Settings > Updates > Check for updates** inside Fuel Ledger and tap **Update app** to update directly without browser warnings.
