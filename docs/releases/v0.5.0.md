Italian translation, livelier motion and an Update app button.

## New

- **Update app:** Settings > Updates > Check for updates asks GitHub for the latest release. When there is a newer one the row becomes **Update app** and opens the APK download; open the file to install it over the current app. Your data is kept.
- **Italian:** the whole app, including form errors, notifications, the widget and CSV import messages, follows the phone's language (Italian or English). On Android 13 and later you can also choose the language per app in the system settings.
- **Motion:** dashboard cards enter in sequence, amounts and the gauge digits count up, charts grow and draw themselves, lists cascade in and collapse when swiped, tabs and detail screens have their own transitions, and the splash draws the ledger lines. Everything stops when system animations are off.

## Changed

- The app now declares the internet permission, used only by the update check. It runs when you tap the row, never in the background, and sends nothing about you or your records.

## Updating
