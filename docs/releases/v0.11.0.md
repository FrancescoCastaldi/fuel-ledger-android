Android Auto support, a cold-start intro video, and voice fill-ups from the car.

## Added

- **Android Auto:** Fuel Ledger appears in Android Auto as a templated app. The overview shows average consumption, this month's cost, cost per kilometre, the latest fill-up and the next deadline.
- **Costs and vehicles in the car:** costs for this month, last month and this year, plus the latest fill-ups; choose the selected vehicle.
- **Fill-ups in the car:** enter odometer, liters and total one field at a time. The app checks them with the same rules as the phone form, including an odometer that must not go backwards.
- **Voice fill-ups:** tap **Voice** and say, for example, "45300 chilometri, 38 litri, 72 euro e 50, pieno". The sentence fills the same fields and opens the confirmation screen. Needs Android 13 or newer on the phone.
- **Intro video:** the cold start plays a short brand video; tap to skip. With system animations turned off, the static splash is shown instead.

## Changed

- Voice entry uses the phone's speech recognizer, which may send the audio to Google. It starts only when you tap **Voice** in the car.

## Upgrading

- Installing this version over 0.10.1 keeps your records.
- To use Android Auto, enable **Unknown sources** in the Android Auto developer settings, because the app is not on Google Play. See [docs/install.md](../install.md).
