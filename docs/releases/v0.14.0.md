A refreshed amber palette, one motion system across the app, and shared transitions that open a list row into its edit form.

## Added

- **Shared transitions:** a refuel, expense or deadline row grows into its edit form, and back.
- **Press feedback:** list rows scale slightly while pressed.

## Changed

- **Palette:** the amber accent is warmer and richer. Night backgrounds are deeper, day surfaces are cleaner, and warning now reads differently from the accent. Text and status colors meet contrast targets.
- **Motion:** tab switches fade through, forms slide along one axis, the gauge needle settles with a small overshoot, and chart bars and the splash share the same easing. Animations still stop when system animations are turned off.

## Upgrading

- Install over 0.13.0 with **Settings > Updates > Check for updates**. Your records are kept.
- No database or cloud schema change in this release.
